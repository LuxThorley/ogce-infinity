How to play

On a phone or tablet: use the joystick in the bottom-left corner to walk, tap the ground to walk there, drag with one finger to turn the camera and pinch to zoom.

🎙️ Voice and typed commands

Hold V or the 🎙️ button and speak, or press / to type. Turn on the wake word in Settings to say “Malcolm, …” hands-free. Only the words you say (as text) are used: the game never records or stores audio.

More keys: J Journal · K Codex · M Malcolm · O Expedition · I Progress & Codex · Enter chat · 5 🔮 Sky & Spirits · 6 👥 Together · U quick-chat wheel · 0 📷 Photo mode. Every game key can be remapped (Settings → Accessibility), with one-handed layouts.

🎥 Camera

Drag (or right-drag) to orbit 360° · mouse wheel or pinch to zoom from over-the-shoulder to a far overview · Z/X rotate · Y/G tilt from ground level up to top-down · L, middle-click or 🎯 recenters behind you · N or 👁️ switches first/third person · 7/8/9 saved views · gamepad right stick. Sensitivity, invert, smoothing, shoulder side, FOV, presets and keys are in Settings → Camera.

⚔️ Duels

Press F (or tap a creature) to start a duel; say “practice” for the training golem. In a duel: F/left-click light attack (chain three) · R/right-click heavy · light-light-heavy = Breaker · hold Shift to block, tap it just before a hit to parry · Space dodge-roll · 1/2 special moves · Esc flee. Orange glow = heavy strike coming. Foes learn your habits, so mix it up!

STRATALux Genesis

Every world is built layer by layer. Light the Lux, climb the strata, and test every idea twice.

You are a newcomer in a fractured, scrambled reality. The strata (SSR layers) have slipped out of order.

  1. Climb the strata. Earn ✨ Lux mostly by understanding: discoveries, puzzles, inventions and wise choices.
  2. Gather the seven core laws (4500 · 12.32 · 72 · 144 · 144000 · 26000 · 365.26) across the worlds, and win over or outwit the Shakespearean characters, each with their own agenda.
  3. Reach the Source Stratum, complete the Realignment and choose which reality to restore: Harmony, Power, Sacrifice… or something stranger.

Track it all on the ✨ Progress screen (I): Lux, Strata reached, Codex, Mastery and Legacy. After the story, New Genesis opens an endless Omniverse.
